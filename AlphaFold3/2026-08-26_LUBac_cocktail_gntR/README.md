## Workflow

Amino acid sequences annotated as gntR domain containing proteins from the 5 LUBac cocktail members, folded in AlphaFold3 with L-lactate, D-lactate, and L- and D-lactate.

* *Veillonella atyica*
* *Coprococcus catus*
* *Anaerostipes hadrus*
* *Anaerostipes caccae*
* *Anaerobutyricum halii*

Determination around predicted specificity will be determined based on placement of the lactate ligand in the competition compared to positive controls.
